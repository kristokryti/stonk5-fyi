How the engine works

Four steps, every round.

1

1% fee on every trade

Half of the fee is paid to a public engine wallet as creator fees.

2

Engine fills up

Every 5 hours, or once the wallet holds 5 SOL — whichever comes first.

3

Buys the top 5

The engine buys the current top 5 StonkFun tokens with the round’s budget.

4

Holders get paid

Tokens are deposited to eligible STONK5 holders. Check what you’re owed at stonk5.com.
